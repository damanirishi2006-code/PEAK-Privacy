Peak Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Peak is a personal-use application designed to provide a customized interface for viewing and understanding data from the user's WHOOP account.

Information Peak Accesses

Peak may access information from the user's WHOOP account through the official WHOOP API, including recovery, sleep, strain, workouts, profile, and body measurement data, depending on the permissions authorized by the user.

How Data Is Used

The data accessed by Peak is used solely to provide the application's features, display the user's information, and provide personalized insights and analysis.

Peak does not sell, rent, or use personal data for advertising.

Data Sharing

Peak does not intentionally share the user's WHOOP data with third parties or other users.

Data Storage

Peak is intended solely for personal use. Data may be processed or stored as necessary for the application to function. The user's data is not intentionally made publicly available.

WHOOP Data

Peak accesses WHOOP data through the official WHOOP Developer Platform after the user has authorized the application.

WHOOP's own privacy policy and terms continue to apply to data handled by WHOOP.

Revoking Access

The user can revoke Peak's access to their WHOOP data through their WHOOP account or applicable application settings.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the functionality of Peak changes.

Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or Peak, please contact the developer through the contact information associated with the Peak project.
